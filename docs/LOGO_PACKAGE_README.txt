Logo package — "The Gateway" (البوابة)                         November 2026

THE IDEA
The M is a gate standing over a road that runs to the horizon (the road is the
negative space under the M). Mithaq means covenant: a pledge kept on every road.

FOLDERS
01_Symbol      The mark alone: colour, reversed (for dark grounds), navy, white, black.
               "small" = one-dash version for sizes below 32 px / 12 mm.
02_Lockups     vertical, horizontal, Arabic-only, English-only — SVG + PNG (transparent)
               + vector PDF for print. All text is converted to outlines.
03_App_Icons   Navy tiles for favicon (32), Apple touch icon (180), apps/social (512).
04_Sector_Icons The eight sector icons (navy/gold and white/gold), 96-unit grid.
05_Patterns    The Gateway band (dark / light / gold) and work-zone chevrons.

CLEAR SPACE & SIZE
x = the width of the M's top stroke (1/5 of the symbol's width).
Keep 1x clear on every side. Minimum: symbol 16 px / 6 mm; horizontal lockup 120 px / 35 mm.

DON'T
Stretch, recolour, rotate, outline, add shadows or gradients, or place the logo
on busy backgrounds. Use the reversed version on navy, asphalt or photos.
